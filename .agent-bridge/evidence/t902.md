## d. 验证/自测

### 1) register 两端口落库

```text
=== ACCEPT-1 after register ===
slot=1 endpoint='tcp://172.16.60.122:9001' com_name='COM11' health_state='online' baud=115200 probe={"status": "ok", "rtt_ms": 12}
slot=2 endpoint='tcp://172.16.60.122:9002' com_name='COM12' health_state='online' baud=115200 probe={"status": "ok", "rtt_ms": 15}
```

结论：完整覆盖 slot/com_name/endpoint/probe_json/health_state/baud。

### 2) 部分 heartbeat 不清空未上报端口

```text
P2_BEFORE {... endpoint: tcp://172.16.60.122:9002, com_name: COM12, ...}
P2_AFTER  {... endpoint: tcp://172.16.60.122:9002, com_name: COM12, ...}
P2_UNCHANGED True
ROW_COUNT 2
```

结论：已确认无需改动（增量 upsert，不删未上报 slot）。

### 3) endpoint 漂移更新 + 空串清空

```text
EP_BEFORE tcp://172.16.60.122:9001
EP_AFTER  tcp://172.16.60.200:9001
NODE_IP   172.16.60.200
EP_EMPTY  None
```

结论：修复前空串不落库；现 `"endpoint" in port` 可更新/清空，且增量未带键不误清。

### 5) 停心跳 181s → stale

（回拨 `last_heartbeat_at`，避免真实 sleep >60s）

```text
NOW 2026-11-21T03:45:52.400784
```
